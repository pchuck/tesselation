# 2-uniform (3⁶; 3⁴.6)₂

`kuniform-n19` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `p6m` · hexagonal lattice

2-uniform k-uniform: the t = 3 flower form of the 3⁴.6 pair — unit triangular lattice with hexagons dissected at the points of the index-9 sublattice ⟨(3,0),(0,3)⟩ (Wikipedia `2-uniform n19`, t = 3, e = 3); 60° hexagonal basis, |b1| = |b2| = 3, det 9√3/2 (right-handed); 13 tiles/cell; A:B = 1:3 vertex orbits pinned by v mod 3; both mirror classes verified exactly, so the wiki's p6 group label under-states the symmetry — this is the achiral p6m form (n20 is the chiral p6 form) (M95)

# Shifted trapezoid courses

`shifted-trapezoid-courses` · family [Wallpaper](../families/pavers.md) (`pavers`) · wallpaper group `pmg` · rectangular lattice

paver tiling: the trapezoid-course row stacked in rows of height 1 per 2×2 cell on the rectangular lattice ⟨(2,0),(0,2)⟩ (det 4), alternate rows reflected in y = 1 and shifted by (1,0): eight trapezoids, two incongruent shapes (areas 3/5 and 2/5) × mirror × row-flip; edge-to-edge (slant slope 1/5); the catalog's first pmg realizer — vertical mirror lines x ∈ ℤ, one horizontal glide family (glide 1, axes y ∈ ℤ), half-turns at (m+1/2, n) for all m, n ∈ ℤ; the (1,0) row shift kills the horizontal mirrors that would make it pmm

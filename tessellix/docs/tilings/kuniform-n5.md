# 2-uniform (3.4.6.4; 3.4².6)

`kuniform-n5` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `p6m` · hexagonal lattice

2-uniform k-uniform: hexagons ringed by squares with triangle fills, two hexagon orbits + two square orbits + one triangle orbit (Wikipedia `2-uniform n5`, t = 5, e = 5); 60° hexagonal basis, |b1| = |b2| = 3+√3, det 9+6√3 (right-handed); 18 tiles/cell; the 3.4².6 vertex orbit is chiral (M90)

# 2-uniform dual (V3.6.3.6; V3².6²)

`kuniform-dual-pmm` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `pmm` · rectangular lattice

face-centroid dual of `kuniform-pmm` (pmm, det 2√3): 1 rhombus (side 2/√3, angles 60°/120°; area 2√3/3) + 2 congruent quadrilaterals (sides 1/√3, 2/√3, 2/√3, √3 in cyclic order; angles 60°/60°/120°/120°; area 2√3/3) per cell, sum 2√3 = |det|, edge-to-edge, four 2-fold centers per cell — (0,0), (1,0), (0,√3/2), (1,√3/2) — all on mirror intersections, mirrors horizontal and vertical through all of them (M106)

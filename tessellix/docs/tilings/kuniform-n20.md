# 2-uniform (3⁶; 3⁴.6)₁

`kuniform-n20` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `p6` · hexagonal lattice

2-uniform k-uniform: flower form — unit triangular lattice with hexagons dissected at the points of an index-13 sublattice, the two-uniform family's first p6 entry (Wikipedia `2-uniform n20`, t = 5; e = 6 exact vs. the wiki's parenthetical 7); 60° hexagonal basis, |b1| = |b2| = √13, det 13√3/2 (right-handed); 21 tiles/cell; A:B = 1:1 vertex orbits pinned by k = 4X+3Y mod 13 (M96)

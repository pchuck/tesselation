# Star and Cross ({8/3})

`star-cross` · family [Islamic](../families/islamic.md) (`islamic`) · wallpaper group `p4m` · square lattice

Islamic star and cross pattern — {8/3} octagram stars with concave crosses, one-parameter family from wide gap to the kissing (khatam) case (p4m). Vertex figures 45.45.135.135 / 45.315 / 90.270 at the kissing radius.

The star is the `{8/3}` octagram — notches at the crossings of the
density-3 chords (each notch lies on the line joining tips `i` and
`i + 3`), notch/tip radius √((2−√2)/2) = 0.541196…, 45° tips. The
`{8/2}` compound of two overlaid squares (notch/tip 0.765368…, 90°
points) is a different figure and a different registry entry
(`pic-4-8-8`); the discrimination is pinned on the emitted tile by
`star_cross_star_is_the_8_3_octagram_not_the_8_2_compound`.

Catalog type: Islamic star-and-cross family (one-parameter).

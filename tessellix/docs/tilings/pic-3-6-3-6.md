# Hankin PIC sixfold khatam

`pic-3-6-3-6` · family [Islamic](../families/islamic.md) (`islamic`) · wallpaper group `p6m` · hexagonal lattice

Hankin polygons-in-contact strapwork on the trihexagonal (3.6.3.6) substrate (contact angle δ = 60°): midpoint-chord walls — {6/2} hexagram in each hexagon, midsegments in each triangle — partitioning the plane into 4 hexagram-core hexagons + 6 hexagram tips + 2 midsegment triangles per cell (a half-scale 3.6.3.6), exact Q(√3)

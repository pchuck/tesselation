# Herringbone 45° (2:1 dominoes)

`herringbone-45` · family [Wallpaper](../families/pavers.md) (`pavers`) · wallpaper group `pgg` · rectangular lattice

paver bond: the herringbone in its 45°-laid canonical frame — chiral single-direction L-pairs of 2:1 dominoes, 2 split dominoes per cell on the rectangular lattice ⟨(0,2√2),(√2,0)⟩ (the same bond as `herringbone`, rotated; long sides split into unit edges) (M115)

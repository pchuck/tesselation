# Trapezoid courses

`trapezoid-courses` · family [Wallpaper](../families/pavers.md) (`pavers`) · wallpaper group `pm` · rectangular lattice

paver tiling: two courses of trapezoids per 2×1 cell on the rectangular lattice ⟨(2,0),(0,1)⟩ (det 2): four trapezoids, two incongruent shapes (areas 3/5 and 2/5) each with its mirror in x = 1, edge-to-edge (slant slope 1/5); the catalog's first pm realizer — vertical mirror lines x = integer, no horizontal mirrors or half-turns (the incongruent prototile classes kill pmm)

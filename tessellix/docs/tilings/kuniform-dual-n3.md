# 2-uniform dual (V4⁴; V3³.4²)₂

`kuniform-dual-n3` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `cmm` · centered lattice

face-centroid dual of `kuniform-n3` (cmm, det 3+√3/2, same centered-rectangular lattice ⟨(1,0),(1/2,3+√3/2)⟩ as the primal): 2 congruent pentagons (same shape as dual-n4) + 2 unit squares (over the two 4⁴ vertex orbits) per cell — 4 faces over the primal's 4 vertex orbits; Σ = 3+√3/2 = |det|, edge-to-edge; cmm inherited with the same off-mirror 2-fold signature at (1/4,3+√3/4), (3/4,3+√3/4) (M145)

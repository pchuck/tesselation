# Glide hexagon strips

`glide-hexagon-strips` · family [Wallpaper](../families/pavers.md) (`pavers`) · wallpaper group `pg` · rectangular lattice

paver tiling: two interlocking hexagon strips per 1×2 cell on the rectangular lattice ⟨(1,0),(0,2)⟩ (det 2): fat (area 29/40) and waist (area 11/40) hexagons, strip 1 the glide image of strip 0 under g(x,y) = (x+1/2, −y) with g² = (1,0); edge-to-edge, five edge directions, no verticals; the catalog's first pg realizer — one horizontal glide family (glide 1/2; axes y ∈ ℤ in two orbits, y ∈ 2ℤ and y = 1+2ℤ), no mirrors, no rotations

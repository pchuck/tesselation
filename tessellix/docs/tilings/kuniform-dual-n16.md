# Dual of 2-uniform (3³.4²; 3².4.3.4)

`kuniform-dual-n16` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `p4g` · square lattice

face-centroid dual of `kuniform-n16` (p4g, det 6+3√3, same 45°-rotated square lattice as the primal): 8 congruent pentagons (the 3².4.3.4 orbit) + 4 congruent pentagons (the 3³.4² orbit) per cell — 12 faces over the primal's 12 vertex orbits, the key-lock tiling; the p4g signature carries over (pure axis mirrors, true diagonal glides) (M121)

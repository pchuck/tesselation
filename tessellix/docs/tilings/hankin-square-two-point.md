# Hankin two-point square weave (4.4.4.4)

`hankin-square-two-point` · family [Islamic](../families/islamic.md) (`islamic`) · wallpaper group `cmm` · centered lattice

Hankin two-point motif inference on the unit square (4.4.4.4) substrate (θK = 45°, split δ = 0.5 — rays leave edge split points and cross): a decagonal star per cell — four corner triangles, two pentagonal petals, one 10-gon core — 7 faces per unit cell ⟨(1,0),(0,1)⟩ (det 1); cmm (2*22) — diagonal mirror families x∓y=k with 2-folds on-mirror at vertices/cell-centers and off-mirror at edge midpoints, no 4-folds (M99)

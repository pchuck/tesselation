# Dual of 2-uniform (4⁴; 3³.4²)₁

`kuniform-dual-n4` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `cmm` · centered lattice

face-centroid dual of `kuniform-n4` (cmm, det 2+√3/2, same centered-rectangular lattice ⟨(1,0),(1/2,2+√3/2)⟩ as the primal): 2 congruent pentagons (over the two 3³.4² vertex orbits; sides 1/√3 ×2, (3+√3)/6 ×2, 1 in cyclic order; area 1/2+√3/4) + 1 unit square (over the 4⁴ vertex orbit; area 1) per cell — 3 faces over the primal's 3 vertex orbits; Σ = 2+√3/2 = |det|, edge-to-edge; cmm inherited — off-mirror 2-folds at (1/4,2+√3/4) and (3/4,2+√3/4), the cmm-vs-pmm discriminator (M145)

# Dual of 2-uniform (3.4².6; 3.6.3.6)₁

`kuniform-dual-n7` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `pmm` · rectangular lattice

face-centroid dual of `kuniform-n7` (pmm, det 2+2√3, same primitive rectangular lattice ⟨(2,0),(0,1+√3)⟩ as the primal): 4 congruent quadrilaterals (over the four 3.4².6 vertex orbits; sides 1, (3+√3)/6, 2√3/3, (1+√3)/2 in cyclic order; angles 90°, 90°, 120°, 60°; area (3+2√3)/6) + 1 rhombus (over the 3.6.3.6 vertex orbit; side 2√3/3, angles 60°/120°; area 2√3/3) per cell — 5 faces over the primal's 5 vertex orbits; Σ = 2+2√3 = |det|, edge-to-edge; pmm inherited — the 2-folds (0, √3/2) and (0, −1/2) sit at crossings of the horizontal and vertical mirrors, the mirror families half a period apart, no true glides

# Hankin sixfold hexagram (3.6.3.6)

`hankin-3-6-3-6` · family [Islamic](../families/islamic.md) (`islamic`) · wallpaper group `p6m` · hexagonal lattice

Hankin greedy-inference motif on the trihexagonal (3.6.3.6) substrate (θK = 60°, δ = 0): each hexagon becomes a regular hexagram — a 12-gon core with 60° tips and 240° notches (vertices alternating on circles of radius 1/2 and √3/2) plus six 60°-lozenge rhombi (7 faces); the two equilateral triangle tiles pair with no contact rays (0 pairs) and stay single faces — 9 faces per cell ⟨(2,0),(1,√3)⟩ (det 2√3), exact Q(√3); θK = 60° chosen over 30°/45° (no star: core tips 120°/90°) and 67.5°/90° (sharper variant / star collapse) for the classical {6/2} hexagram; p6m verified on a ±5-lattice window, D6-exact (M100)

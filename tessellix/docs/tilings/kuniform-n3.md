# 2-uniform (4⁴; 3³.4²)₂

`kuniform-n3` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `cmm` · centered lattice

2-uniform k-uniform: staggered three-square-tall bands alternating with alternating-triangle bands one triangle high — the n4 sibling (Wikipedia `2-uniform n3`, t = 3, e = 5); centered rectangular basis ⟨(1, 0), (1/2, 3+√3/2)⟩ with orthogonal index-2 pair (1, 0), (0, 6+√3), det 3+√3/2 (right-handed); 5 tiles/cell (3 squares + 2 triangles in orbits {rowA, rowC}, {rowB}, {triangles}); 4444 and 33344 vertex orbits, 2 classes/cell each (M108)

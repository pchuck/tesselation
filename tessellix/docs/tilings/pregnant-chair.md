# Pregnant Chairs

`pregnant-chair` · family [Nonperiodic](../families/reptiles.md) (`reptiles`)

λ = √3 two-prototile rep-tile (Bielefeld substitution index, E. Amezcua): the √3-inflated large chair tiles as 1 large + 6 small, the inflated small chair is exactly one large chair (area-conservative); exact Z[√3] integer-pair arithmetic; finite-depth approximant with view-quantized depth, patch-stable under panning/zooming; chiral — 4 of 8 dihedral orientation classes

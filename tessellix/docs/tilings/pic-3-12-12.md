# Hankin PIC twelvefold khatam

`pic-3-12-12` · family [Islamic](../families/islamic.md) (`islamic`) · wallpaper group `p6m` · hexagonal lattice

Hankin polygons-in-contact strapwork on the truncated-hexagonal (3.12.12) substrate (contact angle δ = 60° — the only angle closing both the dodecagon and the triangle): midpoint-chord walls — {12/4} twelvefold rosette in each dodecagon, midsegments in each triangle — partitioning the plane into rosette-core dodecagon + 6 vertex pentagons + 12 band quads + 2 medial triangles + 12 inner quads + 12 star notches per cell, exact Q(√3)

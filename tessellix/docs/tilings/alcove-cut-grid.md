# Alcove-cut grid

`alcove-cut-grid` · family [Wallpaper](../families/pavers.md) (`pavers`) · wallpaper group `p3m1` · hexagonal lattice

paver tiling: side-2 triangular grid on the index-3 sublattice in which every grid triangle is cut from its alcove point — the orbit of one generic Q(√3) junction under the edge-line mirrors of the up-triangle — to its three vertices, per hexagonal cell ⟨(3,−√3),(3,√3)⟩ (det 6√3): 18 tiles, three congruence classes (each mirror-closed, achiral as a set); edge-to-edge (1350 interior edges, 0 T-junctions); the catalog's p3m1 realizer — the *333 fingerprint (all three rot120 centre orbits on mirrors, the contrast to M84's p31m one-orbit-on-mirrors), mirrors in the 0°/60°/120° families, no 60°/180°/300° rotations, no glides; closes the last gap of the 17-group wallpaper catalog (M85, HX-STUDY handoff)

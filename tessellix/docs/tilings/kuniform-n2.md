# 2-uniform (3.12.12; 3.4.3.12)

`kuniform-n2` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `p4m` · square lattice

2-uniform k-uniform: dodecagons kissing flat-edge-to-flat-edge on a square lattice, unit squares and edge triangles filling each four-dodecagon hole (Wikipedia `2-uniform n=2`, t = 2, e = 3); square basis ⟨(2+√3,0),(0,2+√3)⟩, det 7+4√3; 6 tiles/cell (1 dodecagon + 1 square + 4 triangles); origin a dodecagon center = 4-fold center (M118)

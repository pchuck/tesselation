# 2-uniform (3⁶; 3³.4²)₁

`kuniform-n14` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `pmm` · rectangular lattice

2-uniform k-uniform: single square rows alternating with double triangle strips — two counterposed unit-triangle rows, strip height √3 (Wikipedia `2-uniform n14`, t = 3, e = 4); rectangular (primitive) basis ⟨(1, 0), (0, 1+√3)⟩, det 1+√3 (right-handed); 5 tiles/cell (1 square + 4 triangles in three orbits {strip1-up, strip2-down}, {strip1-down, strip2-up}); 3⁶ vertex orbit 1 class/cell, 33344 2 classes/cell (M103)

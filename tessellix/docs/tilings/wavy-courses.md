# Wavy courses

`wavy-courses` · family [Wallpaper](../families/pavers.md) (`pavers`) · wallpaper group `cm` · centered lattice

paver tiling: zigzag courses of height 4/3 (waves of slope ±1/3, peaks 1/3 above the valleys) per 2×2 cell on the centered-rectangular lattice ⟨(1,1),(2,0)⟩ (det 2; the x+y-even lattice, also square-metric): each column-course quadrilateral split by a diagonal seam alternating slope +1/−1 with column–course parity — four triangles, two incongruent shapes (areas 1/3 and 2/3, squared edges {4,10,18} vs {10,16,18} ×9); edge-to-edge; the catalog's first cm realizer — mirror lines x = integer (all of them), glides (2j+1−x, y+1) on the half-integer axes with glide (0,1), no half-turns, no horizontal mirrors; the lattice's square metric is a degenerate centered-rectangular realization

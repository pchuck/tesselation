# Basketweave three-brick (2:1 dominoes)

`basketweave-3` · family [Wallpaper](../families/pavers.md) (`pavers`) · wallpaper group `pmm` · rectangular lattice

paver bond: three courses of horizontal 2:1 dominoes stacked without offset, then one course of vertical dominoes, repeating with vertical period 5 — 15 split dominoes per cell on the rectangular lattice ⟨(6,0),(0,5)⟩ (an index-3 superlattice of the true ⟨(2,0),(0,5)⟩, kept for the whole-cluster cell convention) (M116)

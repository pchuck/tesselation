# 2-uniform (3³.4²; 3².4.3.4)₁

`kuniform-n16` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `p4g` · square lattice

2-uniform k-uniform: staggered square rows and triangle bands in the snub-square-related form (Wikipedia `2-uniform n=16`, t = 4, e = 5 — 4-isohedral, 5-isotoxal); 45°-rotated square basis ⟨(h,−h),(h,h)⟩ with h = (3+√3)/2, det 2h² = 6+3√3; 18 tiles/cell (2 square orbits + 2 triangle orbits); the diagonal cosets are true glides — the p4g signature, torus census 4 tile orbits (8+4+4+2) + 5 edge orbits (M119)

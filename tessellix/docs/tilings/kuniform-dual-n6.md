# Dual of 2-uniform (3.4².6; 3.6.3.6)₂

`kuniform-dual-n6` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `cmm` · centered lattice

face-centroid dual of `kuniform-n6` (cmm, det 2+2√3, same centered-rectangular lattice ⟨(2,0),(1,1+√3)⟩ as the primal): 4 congruent quadrilaterals (over the four 3.4².6 vertex orbits; sides 1, (3+√3)/6, 2√3/3, (1+√3)/2 in cyclic order; angles 90°, 90°, 120°, 60°; area (3+2√3)/6) + 1 rhombus (over the 3.6.3.6 vertex orbit; side 2√3/3, angles 60°/120°; area 2√3/3) per cell — 5 faces over the primal's 5 vertex orbits; Σ = 2+2√3 = |det|, edge-to-edge; cmm inherited — 2mm points where the horizontal and vertical mirrors cross at (1/2, √3/2) and (3/2, √3/2), off-mirror 2-folds at (1, −1/2) and (2, −1/2), the cmm-vs-pmm discriminator

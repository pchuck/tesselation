# 2-uniform (4⁴; 3³.4²)₁

`kuniform-n4` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `cmm` · centered lattice

2-uniform k-uniform: staggered two-square-tall bands alternating with alternating-triangle bands one triangle high (Wikipedia `2-uniform n4`, t = 2, e = 4); centered rectangular basis ⟨(1, 0), (1/2, 2+√3/2)⟩ with orthogonal index-2 pair (1, 0), (0, 4+√3), det 2+√3/2 (right-handed); 4 tiles/cell (2 squares + 2 triangles); vertex orbits 4444 (1 class/cell) + 33344 (2 classes/cell) (M103)

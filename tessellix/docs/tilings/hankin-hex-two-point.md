# Hankin two-point hex weave (6³)

`hankin-hex-two-point` · family [Islamic](../families/islamic.md) (`islamic`) · wallpaper group `p6m` · hexagonal lattice

Hankin two-point motif inference on the unit hexagon (θK = 45°, split δ = 0.5 — rays leave edge split points and cross): 12 interlaced bracket-and-diamond faces around a central dodecagon per cell ⟨(3/2,√3/2),(0,√3)⟩ (det 3√3/2); Kaplan Fig 3.6 row 1 col c (M94)

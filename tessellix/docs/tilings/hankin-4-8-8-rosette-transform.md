# Hankin rosette-transform khatam (4.8.8)

`hankin-4-8-8-rosette-transform` · family [Islamic](../families/islamic.md) (`islamic`) · wallpaper group `p4m` · square lattice

Kaplan rosette transform (§3.8) of the 4.8.8 substrate at θK = 45° — each octagon maps to a concentric inner octagon + 8 pentagons (r′ radius, spokes = half inner side), each square to 4 half-squares (bisectors meet in a centre cluster) — then Hankin motif inference (θE = 45°) on the transformed table: 16 triangles, 36 quads, 32 pentagons, 8 hexagons and one 16-gon per cell ⟨(1+√2,0),(0,1+√2)⟩ (det 3+2√2), exact Q(√2); the cell table is p4m-exact to 6.5e-16 (M99)

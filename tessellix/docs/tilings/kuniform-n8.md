# 2-uniform (3.4.6.4; 3³.4²)

`kuniform-n8` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `p6m` · hexagonal lattice

2-uniform k-uniform: the second p6m 3.4.6.4 form — hexagons ringed by squares with triangle fills, one square–square edge per cell, 2-folds at square–square edge midpoints (cf. n9's triangle–triangle) (Wikipedia `2-uniform n8`, t = 4, e = 4); same basis class as n9 (det 6+7√3/2); origin a hexagon center; 15 tiles/cell (M89)

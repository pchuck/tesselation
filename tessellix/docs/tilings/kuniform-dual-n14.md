# Dual of 2-uniform (3⁶; 3³.4²)₁

`kuniform-dual-n14` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `pmm` · rectangular lattice

face-centroid dual of `kuniform-n14` (pmm, det 1+√3, same primitive rectangular lattice ⟨(1,0),(0,1+√3)⟩ as the primal): 2 congruent pentagons (over the two 3³.4² vertex orbits; sides 1/√3 ×2, (3+√3)/6 ×2, 1 in cyclic order; area 1/2+√3/4) + 1 regular hexagon (over the 3⁶ vertex orbit; side 1/√3, area √3/2) per cell — 3 faces over the primal's 3 vertex orbits; Σ = 1+√3 = |det|, edge-to-edge; pmm inherited — all four 2-folds on the mirror intersections, no off-mirror 2-folds, no true glides (M147)

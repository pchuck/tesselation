# Hankin sixfold rhombitrihex motif (3.4.6.4)

`hankin-3-4-6-4` · family [Islamic](../families/islamic.md) (`islamic`) · wallpaper group `p6m` · hexagonal lattice

Hankin greedy-inference motif on the rhombitrihexagonal (3.4.6.4) substrate (θK = 60°, δ = 0): each hexagon becomes a regular hexagram — a 12-gon core with 60° tips and 240° notches plus six 60°-lozenge rhombi (7 faces); the three unit squares pair with no contact rays (0 pairs) and stay single faces; each equilateral triangle tile sits in the side-1/2 4-subdivision limit (its star tips reach the edge midpoints exactly — 4 faces) — 18 faces per cell ⟨(1.5+√3/2, √3/2+1/2),(0,1+√3)⟩ (det 4√3/2+3), exact Q(√3); θK = 60° chosen over 30° (30° slivers), 45° (no hexagon star; asymmetric triangle motifs), 67.5° (sharper, 45° tips), and 90° (star collapse) for the classical {6/2} hexagram; p6m verified on a ±5-lattice window, D6-exact (M100)

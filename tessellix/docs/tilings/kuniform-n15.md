# 2-uniform (3⁶; 3³.4²)₂

`kuniform-n15` · family [Two-Uniform](../families/kuniform.md) (`kuniform`) · wallpaper group `cmm` · centered lattice

2-uniform k-uniform: staggered square rows alternating with triple triangle strips — three unit-triangle rows, every horizontal edge shared back-to-back, square rows flanked by one triangle orbit (Wikipedia `2-uniform n15`, t = 4, e = 5); centered rectangular basis ⟨(1, 0), (1/2, 1+3√3/2)⟩ with orthogonal index-2 pair (1, 0), (0, 2+3√3), det 1+3√3/2 (right-handed); 7 tiles/cell (1 square + 6 triangles in orbits {flanker pair}, {yellow pair}, {cyan pair}); 3⁶ and 33344 vertex orbits, 2 classes/cell each (M108)
